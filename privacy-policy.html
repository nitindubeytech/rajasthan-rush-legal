Privacy Policy

Rajasthan Rush · Daksh Studio

Summary

Rajasthan Rush is a free game made by Daksh Studio. It works offline and has no accounts. We do not ourselves collect, store on our servers, share or sell your personal information. The Android app shows ads provided by Google AdMob, which may collect some data as explained under Third parties below.

What is saved on your device

To let you continue playing, the game saves the following only on your own device: the rider name and avatar you choose, your coins, rides, unlocked items, level progress, best score and your settings (sound, graphics, controls, time of day).

This information never leaves your device. We cannot see it, and we cannot recover it for you.

What we do not collect

We do not ourselves collect your real name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data. The game does not ask for any special permissions.

Ads and third parties

The Android app shows banner and full-screen ads using Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, and information about your device and how you interact with ads. Ads may be personalised unless you opt out. Where required (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK) the app asks for your consent first.

You can reset your advertising ID or turn off ad personalisation in your phone's settings (Settings > Google > Ads). Learn more at policies.google.com/technologies/ads. The game contains no other analytics or social-media software. If you choose to share the game or rate it, your phone opens its own share sheet or the Google Play Store, which are governed by their own privacy policies.

Children

Rajasthan Rush is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Please choose a rider name that does not reveal a child's full real name.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game, or clearing the app's storage in your phone settings, permanently deletes everything the game has saved. You can also reset best score and settings inside the game. To remove data held by Google for ads, use your Google account's ad settings.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change how the game handles data, we will update this policy and the date below before the change takes effect.
