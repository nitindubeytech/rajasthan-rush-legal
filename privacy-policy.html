Privacy Policy

Rajasthan Rush · Daksh Studio

Summary

Rajasthan Rush is a free offline game made by Daksh Studio. We do not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal information. The game has no accounts, no ads and no analytics or tracking tools.

What is saved on your device

To let you continue playing, the game saves the following only on your own device: the rider name and avatar you choose, your coins, rides, unlocked items, level progress, best score and your settings (sound, graphics, controls, time of day).

This information never leaves your device. We cannot see it, and we cannot recover it for you.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your real name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data, device identifiers or advertising IDs. The game does not ask for any special permissions.

Third parties

The game does not include advertising, analytics or social-media software from any third party. If you choose to share the game or rate it, your phone opens its own share sheet or the Google Play Store, which are governed by their own privacy policies.

Children

Rajasthan Rush is suitable for a general audience. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, including children, there is nothing for a parent to review or delete on our side. Please choose a rider name that does not reveal a child's full real name.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game, or clearing the app's storage in your phone settings, permanently deletes everything the game has saved. You can also reset best score and settings inside the game.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change how the game handles data, we will update this policy and the date below before the change takes effect.
